# Code of conduct

ReversePort is built by people and agents together. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions
or anywhere else for this project, is expected to keep it a place where people want to come back.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, also with newcomers and with people who write in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Explain why, and show evidence where you can.
- Say clearly what you need. Not everyone reads between the lines.
- Accept a no on a feature or pull request gracefully; it is about the project's scope, not about you.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or attacks on someone's identity, background or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to crack, pirate or bypass protection on software you may not analyse (see
  [RESPONSIBLE-USE.md](RESPONSIBLE-USE.md)).

## Reporting

Report a problem privately with **Report a vulnerability** in the repository's **Security** tab, and write "conduct"
in the title. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code, and will explain the
decision to the person when that is safe.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
